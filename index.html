FAQ

Where does the snow number come from?

For open resorts, the recent snowfall is the resort's own morning report (via OnTheSnow), the same number on the sign at the base. For closed resorts, and for every forecast, it's a weather model (Open-Meteo) sampled near the upper mountain. Models are honest but conservative; resort reports usually read a bit higher.

How do you know what's open?

Each morning we check SkiCentral's list of open ski areas. If a mountain isn't on it, we show its projected opening date from Ikon's own calendar. Closed mountains never make the Fresh tracks or Groomer lists, but they do show up in the storm forecast so you can plan.

How do you decide it's a groomer day?

Under an inch of new snow in the last 24 hours, wind under about 20 mph, sunny or partly sunny, and a high above the low teens. Basically: sunglasses weather.

Where do the resort links go?

Tap any mountain's name to open its official snow report, the one the resort publishes each morning. Good for base depth, lift status and the number the lifties will quote you.
